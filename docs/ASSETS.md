# أصول الهوية

## بطاقة المشاركة الاجتماعية

- الملف الإنتاجي: `apps/web/public/og.png`
- المقاس: `1200×630`
- الاستخدام: Open Graph وTwitter/X والروابط المشتركة التي لا تملك صورة عقار خاصة.
- الإنشاء: مولّد الصور المدمج في Codex، ثم تحسين PNG محلياً باستخدام Sharp دون تغيير التصميم.

### أمر الإنشاء الأصلي

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: Open Graph social card
Primary request: Create exactly one premium, modern Omani-inspired brand card for BHD R real estate management, with a clean editorial identity and strong digital readability.
Scene/backdrop: Deep forest-green field with restrained layered geometry; abstract patterns inspired by Omani architectural arches, carved screens, and stepped masonry, interpreted as original nonliteral geometric forms.
Style/medium: polished high-end flat graphic design with subtle dimensional layering, crisp vector-like edges, refined luxury, minimal visual noise.
Composition/framing: landscape 1200×630 pixels or the nearest supported 40:21 aspect ratio; balanced central typographic hierarchy; generous negative space; keep every text element and important motif within a safe inner margin of at least 8% on all sides for social-platform cropping.
Lighting/mood: sophisticated, calm, trustworthy, premium; subtle controlled highlights only.
Color palette: use only deep green #092D24, turquoise #08A39F, blue #174B70, bronze-gold #B58D55, and warm ivory #F4F0E8; ensure high contrast and accessible text legibility.
Typography: modern refined bilingual sans serif; render the title large and the bilingual descriptor clearly beneath it. Preserve Arabic joining and letterforms correctly. Render the two requested text elements exactly once each.
Text (verbatim):
"BHD R"
"إدارة العقارات | Real Estate Management"
Text accuracy: The first line must read exactly B-H-D, one space, R. The second line must read exactly إدارة العقارات, one space, vertical bar, one space, Real Estate Management. No substitutions, no added punctuation, no misspellings, no extra words.
Constraints: one finished social card only; clean high-contrast layout; crop-safe; original abstract Omani architectural geometry; no people; no photographs; no flags; no government emblems; no government logos; no seals; no coats of arms; no unrelated branding.
Avoid: any text beyond the two verbatim strings; watermarks; signatures; mockup frames; browser chrome; device frames; ornamental clutter; low contrast; tiny text; generic Islamic iconography; photorealistic buildings.
```

تستخدم صفحات العقارات والوحدات صورة السجل الأساسية عند توفرها، وتزيل صفحات الفواتير والصفحات غير المتاحة بيانات الصور الموروثة صراحةً لمنع أي تسريب أو معاينة مضللة.
